I am a PhD researcher at the Center for Digital Music ([C4DM](https://www.c4dm.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/)), Queen Mary University of London, supervised by [Prof. Simon Dixon](https://www.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/~simond/). I am grateful to be mentored by [Prof. Eran Egozy](https://mta.mit.edu/person/eran-egozy). Prior to joining C4DM, I was a Senior Research Assistant at Singapore University of Technology and Design, under the supervision of [Prof. Dorien Herremans](https://istd.sutd.edu.sg/faculty/dorien-herremans/) and [Prof. Ernest Chong](https://istd.sutd.edu.sg/people/faculty/ernest-chong). My research is driven by my passion about technology, sound and music. My current research is centered around AI for music. I am the first author (or co-first-author) of [Mustango](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.08355), [Moonbeam](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.15559) and [GAPS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.08653).
