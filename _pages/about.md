I am a PhD researcher at the Centre for Digital Music ([C4DM](https://www.c4dm.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/)), Queen Mary University of London, supervised by [Prof. Simon Dixon](https://www.eecs.qmul.ac.uk/~simond/). I am grateful to be mentored by [Prof. Eran Egozy](https://mta.mit.edu/person/eran-egozy). My research centres on multimodal foundation models for music. During my PhD I have been a Research Scientist Intern at Meta and at Spotify. Before joining C4DM, I was a Senior Research Assistant at the Singapore University of Technology and Design, supervised by [Prof. Dorien Herremans](https://istd.sutd.edu.sg/faculty/dorien-herremans/) and [Prof. Ernest Chong](https://istd.sutd.edu.sg/people/faculty/ernest-chong).
